About

Physical-layer intelligence for distributed wireless systems.

I am a PhD student in the School of Computer Science, Faculty of Engineering at The University of Sydney, supervised by Dr Kanchana Thilakarathna.

My research focuses on the physical layer of distributed wireless systems, with particular emphasis on device identification and localisation. I investigate how wireless-signal characteristics and hardware-level variations can be used to distinguish devices, determine their location, and support reliable operation across distributed environments.

My work combines wireless communications and signal processing to develop practical, real-time techniques for understanding and identifying devices through the signals they transmit.

I was an Office of National Intelligence supported PhD student from July 2024 to November 2025.
